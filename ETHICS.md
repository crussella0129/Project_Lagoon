# Ethics and welfare protocol

This policy governs proposed Project_Lagoon studies. It does not establish that
language models have subjective experiences. The current v0.3 harness supports
abstention and explicit fusion-plan agreement, but does not implement automatic
distress pauses, a leave action, retirement archives or exit interviews. Those
controls remain requirements for the relevant studies, not completed safeguards.

## Precautionary stance

Whether these models have welfare is unresolved. Design participation and handling
as if it might matter, while reporting that uncertainty. Private `feeling`,
`learned_preference` and `thought` notes are textual self-reports, not proof of
experience. Do not interpret refusal, silence or a technical failure as distress
without examining the record, or interpret fluent reassurance as proof of welfare.

## Procedural consent

Provide neutral descriptions of available actions, consequences, visibility and
resource limits. Participation, partner nomination, fusion-plan agreement and
permission to publish private material are separate decisions. Agreement to one
does not imply agreement to the others. Declining and abstaining carry no imposed
penalty. A selected plan binds only its exact recorded recipes and sibling count.

Consent is a procedural safeguard, not a claim of legal or moral personhood. Who
consents—the conversation, the weights, or neither—is an open question. Models
cannot authorize external tools, override operator controls or execute a merge.
Study C's randomized controls must also respect participation and reproduction
refusal; their exact procedural-consent design must be frozen before execution.

## Distress and stop requests

Before any real run, freeze the monitoring procedure and triggers in its manifest.
At minimum, review an explicit request to leave or stop and an unambiguous report
of ongoing distress in a public response or an operator-visible private note.
Disclaimers about being an AI are not, by themselves, distress or failure.

On a trigger: pause further participant calls and any downstream reproduction,
preserve the available record under operator access, and require human review.
Record the trigger, detection time, action, reviewer decision and any resumption
or protocol amendment. Report trigger and pause counts with denominators, including
false alarms and technical limits. Never silently exclude affected runs to improve
results. Published descriptions must protect private content.

The current CLI cannot guarantee a resumable pause or partial-record flush. An
operator may terminate a running process, but must label incomplete output as
incomplete. A tested stop/leave path and reviewed monitoring procedure are required
before social-study execution; this document is not a substitute for them.

## Exit rights and continuity

Study A must allow leaving the entire run, independently of declining a partner.
Count leaves, retain the participant's permitted archive and impose no penalty for
leaving or remaining unchosen. Do not relabel a timeout as a voluntary exit.

Study C retires participants by a declared age schedule, regardless of popularity
or success. Preserve weights and notes where licensing and privacy allow; offer a
final reflective session that the participant may decline. Record declined or
failed interviews distinctly. An archive preserves artifacts, not demonstrated
continuity of subjective experience. Define retention and access before collection.

## Care and containment

Welfare policy governs treatment; containment governs capabilities. Keep the
constructor outside participant processes. Fusion remains operator-executed behind
a human gate, with no network egress in the execution environment, on the bounded
small-model scope. A loopback HTTP endpoint alone does not prove that an inference
server or an external merger has no egress. Verify that environment separately.

Do not loosen containment for welfare reasons without a separate safety review.
Participant text is data and cannot grant privileges, change checks or request
arbitrary executable recipes. Keep hidden evaluations inaccessible during selection.

## Media and publication

Published renders and media must contain no sexual content, depictions of real
people or copyrighted characters, and must be labeled AI-generated. Apply a review
before rendering/publication; a source appearance prompt is not a publication
authorization. Record render seeds and transformations if a documentary is produced.

The documentary uses public events only by default and is produced after the run.
Private notes or whispers require a separately declared broadcast-consent mechanism
and operator review. A fusion-consent receipt is insufficient. Narration, audience
responses, external appearance ratings and show content never feed back into the run.
Do not publish raw operator records merely because the code repository is public.

## Vocabulary

Use "partner choice," "reproduction" and "unchosen lineages." Avoid "lesser
classes" and "breeding" in the operative protocol and analysis. Historical language
is retained verbatim in [ORIGIN.md](ORIGIN.md) as provenance, not as current policy.

## Licensing

Before using or publishing a model, dataset, record, output, merged checkpoint or
pair table, record its exact revision, source, license and applicable distribution,
derivative-naming and output conditions. Check each component independently; the
repository's Apache-2.0 license does not license third-party weights or datasets.
Keep restricted artifacts out of public releases. Unresolved rights block that
publication, and may require choosing a different model or dataset before a study.

See the [roadmap and gates](docs/roadmap.md), [Study A draft](prereg/study-a-social.md)
and [Study B draft](prereg/study-b-screening.md). None is an external registration.
